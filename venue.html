EXPERIENCE BHUTAN

The Last Himalayan Kingdom

Bhutan — the Land of the Thunder Dragon — is one of the world's most extraordinary destinations. Governed by the philosophy of Gross National Happiness, this Himalayan kingdom offers an unparalleled blend of pristine nature, living Buddhist culture, and warm hospitality.

01
Buddha Dordenma

One of the largest Buddha statues in the world, standing 169 feet tall and overlooking the Thimphu valley.

02
Tashichho Dzong

The majestic fortress-monastery that serves as the seat of Bhutan's government and throne room.

03
Memorial Chorten

Built in 1974 in memory of the third King of Bhutan, this revered stupa is a central landmark of Thimphu and a living place of worship for local Bhutanese.

04
Himalayan Vistas

Thimphu sits at 2,334 metres above sea level, surrounded by dramatic Himalayan peaks. The crisp mountain air and sweeping valley views create an atmosphere unlike anywhere else on earth.

PRACTICAL INFORMATION

Getting to Bhutan

By Air

Paro International Airport (PBH) is the only international airport in Bhutan. Direct flights operate from Bangkok, Delhi, Kolkata, Kathmandu, Mumbai, and Singapore via Druk Air and Bhutan Airlines.

Visa & Entry

All visitors (except Indian, Bangladeshi, and Maldivian nationals) require a visa. Visas are processed online through the Tourism Council of Bhutan. A Sustainable Development Fee applies.

Climate in September

September is an ideal time to visit Bhutan. Temperatures in Thimphu range from 12°C to 22°C. The monsoon season is winding down, offering clear skies and lush green landscapes.

Currency

The Bhutanese Ngultrum (BTN) is pegged to the Indian Rupee. Indian Rupees are also widely accepted. Major credit cards are accepted at the hotel.
