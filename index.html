ABOUT THE CONGRESS

A Premier Forum for Cardiovascular Excellence

The South Asia Cardiovascular Meet 2026 brings together leading cardiologists, internists, and cardiovascular specialists from across the South Asian region for three days of scientific exchange, clinical education, and collegial collaboration.

Set against the breathtaking backdrop of the Himalayas in Thimphu, Bhutan, this congress offers a unique confluence of world-class science and cultural immersion — an experience that nourishes both the mind and the spirit.

Evidence-Based Updates

The latest clinical evidence and guideline updates in cardiovascular medicine.

Expert Insights & Discussions

Interactive panel discussions with regional and international thought leaders.

Practical Clinical Takeaways

Case-based learning and practical tools you can apply immediately in practice.

Regional Collaboration

Networking opportunities that strengthen South Asian cardiovascular care networks.

Hypertension

VENUE & DESTINATION

The Pema by Realm, Thimphu

Nestled in the heart of Thimphu, the capital of Bhutan, The Pema by Realm offers a world-class hospitality experience infused with authentic Bhutanese elegance. Surrounded by pristine Himalayan landscapes, it provides the perfect setting for a congress of this distinction.

EXPERIENCE BHUTAN
Buddha Dordenma
The magnificent 51.5-metre gilded bronze Buddha statue overlooking the Thimphu valley.
Tashichho Dzong
The majestic fortress-monastery that serves as the seat of Bhutan's government and throne room.
Memorial Chorten
A sacred stupa built in memory of the third King of Bhutan, a spiritual landmark of Thimphu.
Himalayan Views
Breathtaking panoramas of the Eastern Himalayas, prayer flags, and pristine mountain air.
