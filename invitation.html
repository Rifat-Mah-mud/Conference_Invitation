Chairperson's Invitation

Dear Colleagues,

It is our great pleasure to invite you to the South Asia Cardiovascular Meet 2026, to be held from 19–21 September 2026 at The Pema by Realm, Thimphu, Bhutan.

This meeting has been conceived as a premier regional scientific forum bringing together eminent cardiologists and cardiovascular physicians from across South Asia to exchange knowledge, discuss emerging evidence, and strengthen regional collaboration in cardiovascular medicine.

With a scientific program focused on Hypertension, Dyslipidemia, Heart Failure, Preventive Cardiology, and Contemporary Cardiovascular Practice, the meeting aims to facilitate meaningful discussions that can translate into improved patient outcomes throughout the region.

Set against the serene beauty of the Himalayas and Bhutan's rich cultural heritage, we hope this gathering will foster new friendships, inspire collaborative research, and encourage the sharing of practical clinical experiences among colleagues from neighboring countries.

We look forward to welcoming you to Thimphu.

Warm regards,

Chairperson
